
***Pharmacology*** is the science of drugs.
A medication is a substance used to :
-  Prevent or treat disease
-  Relieve pain

***Pharmacodynamics*** is a process by which medication works on the body.

| Word                            | Definition                                                                  |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Agonist                         | Causes stimulation of receptors                                             |
| Antagonist                      | Binds to a receptor and blocks other medications or chemicals(Ex: *Narcan*) |
| Dose                            | Amount of medication given(Depends on weight, age, desired action)          |
| Action                          | Therapeutic effect(Desired or intended effect)                              |
| Indications                     | Reasons or conditions medication is given                                   |
| Contraindications               | Harmful/no positive effects                                                 |
| Contraindications-Absolute      | Situation which medications should never be given                           |
| Contraindications-Relative      | Benefits outweigh risks                                                     |
| Side Effects                    | Actions other than desired ones                                             |
| Side Effects-Unintended Effects | Undesirable but little risk                                                 |
| Side Effects-Untoward Effects   | Can be harmful                                                              |
| **Pharmacokinetics**            | Actions of the body upon medication or chemical                             |
| Onset of action                 | Time from administration until clinical effect occurs                       |
| Duration                        | Length of time effects persist                                              |
| Elimination                     | How medications/chemicals are removed from body                             |
| Peak                            | Point or period when maximum clinical effect is achieved                    |

#### Medications Names
The ***generic name*** is simple, clear, non-proprietary name.
-  Example: *acetaminophen*
The ***trade name*** is a manufacturer's brand name.
-  Begins with a capital letter
-  <u>One drug may have more than one trade name</u>
	-  Example: *Tylenol*

-  Prescription medications
-  Over-the-counter (**OTC**) medications
-  Recreational drugs
	-  Taken for pleasure, illegal drugs
-  Herbal remedies
-  Enhancement drugs
-  Vitamin supplements
