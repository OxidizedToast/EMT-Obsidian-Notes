
#### **Will learn how to**
-  Give medicine
-  Different routes to give medicines
-  Why we give medicines
-  When we don't give medicines

#### **Intro**
Medications are an important intervention. Medications may alleviate pain and improve the patient's condition. <span style="color: var(--color-red)">Failure</span> to <u>administrate medications safely and competently</u> can lead to serious consequences for the patient, including <span style="color: var(--color-red)">death</span>.
	**As an EMT, you will:**
		  - Administer medications
		  - Help patients self-administer medications
		  - Ask patients about medication use
		  - Report patient information to hospital personnel
